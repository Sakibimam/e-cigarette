V A P O R

a virtual cigarette, tracked by your hands and mouth

  1. Put any two fingers together — thumb and index, index and middle, whichever — and reach into a cigarette on the right. The ring shows where it will be taken from.
  2. Bring it to your lips and purse your mouth — the ember flares as you draw
  3. Take it away from your mouth and open / funnel your lips — real smoke pours out
  4. Release the pinch at your lips to let it hang there hands-free, or spread your hand wide open to let it go
  5. After a few drags the ash builds up — flick your hand or tap it with your other finger to knock it off
